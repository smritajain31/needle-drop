Needle Drop

Sashiko is a Japanese running stitch, sewn slowly and on purpose. Here your hands do the work. Waving doesn't stitch. Each stitch takes four deliberate moves, just like the real thing.

1Come upPinch to hold the needle, rest it on the glowing dot
2LayDraw the thread along the line
3Go downHold still on the ring
4PullPull away until it's snug

Your camera feed is read on this device to find your hands. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Your other open hand steadies the hoop.
